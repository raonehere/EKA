EKA Forest: a forest tent stay in Vattavada, Munnar, Kerala

What is
EKA Forest

EKA Forest is a campground and eco-venue in Vattavada, near Munnar, set among the highlands and shaped by a close connection to nature. It brings people together to stay, gather, learn, and share meals while enjoying the outdoors and the slower rhythm of life here. With comfortable tents, a garden, thoughtfully curated workshops and retreats, and home-style meals from the farm kitchen, it’s a place to appreciate the landscape, feel at home in nature, and enjoy a gentler rhythm of living.

Come to EKA Forest to step away from the noise and rush of everyday life. Explore the village, trek through the Meadows, and discover nearby waterfalls at your own pace. As evening settles, enjoy a beautiful sunset and gather around a cozy campfire on a lovely, chilly day.

Stays & experiences

Photo: forest tent exterior

Forest tents

Tents are set within sturdy protective frames across the terraced landscape, with mountain views, warm bedding for chilly nights, and clean common washrooms.

Photo: bonfire night

Campfire & stargazing

Nights in Vattavada are mostly cold and clear. Spend your evenings around the campfire. On clear nights, look up and take in the starry skies.

Photo: Vattavada farm terraces

Farms & village walks

Walk past terraced vegetable farms, garlic fields and strawberry patches, and through the old Tamil village of Kovilur and its lanes.

Photo: shola forest trail

Shola & viewpoints

Pambadum Shola National Park is a short drive away, while Top Station, about 30 km from Eka, offers sweeping views of the valleys and hills beyond. Visit early in the morning to catch the clouds rolling through the valley.

How to reach?

EKA Forest is in Vattavada, about 45 km (roughly 2 hours) from Munnar. Drive past Mattupetty, Kundala and Top Station, then through Pambadum Shola National Park. The forest check-post only lets vehicles through between 6 AM and 6 PM, so plan to arrive before dark.

  • By air: Cochin International Airport (about 160 km, around 5 hours)
  • By train: Aluva or Ernakulam Junction, then a taxi or bus to Munnar
  • By road: Munnar → Kovilur (Vattavada). Taxis and jeeps run from Munnar town. Take a jeep or auto from Kovilur town to EKA (20 minutes drive).

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly is EKA Forest?

In Vattavada village, Devikulam taluk, Idukki district, Kerala (PIN 685615), about 45 km north-east of Munnar near the Kerala–Tamil Nadu border. We're roughly 4–5 km from Pambadum Shola National Park and 13 km from Top Station. We'll share an exact map pin when you book.

How do I book a stay?

Message us on WhatsApp or send a DM on Instagram @eka_forest_ with your dates and number of guests. We'll confirm availability, rates and advance payment details.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 1 PM to 6 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Because the Pambadum Shola check-post closes at 6 PM, please plan to cross it before then.

What is the best time to visit Vattavada?

September to March is the most popular time, with clear skies and green valleys. December and January are the coldest and mistiest months, and nights can get close to freezing. Vattavada sits in a rain-shadow area, so it gets far less rain than Munnar, even during the monsoon.

How cold does it get, and what should I pack?

Days are pleasant, but nights are cold all year and can drop to single digits (°C) in winter. Bring a warm jacket, layers, socks, a cap, comfortable walking shoes, a torch, and any medicines you need.

Is food available at the property?

Yes. We serve simple home-style meals (breakfast, lunch and dinner on request) in our outdoor dining area, often made with vegetables from Vattavada's farms. Let us know about dietary preferences when you book.

Are the bathrooms attached to the tents?

We have clean common washrooms.

Are pets and kids welcome?

Yes, families and well-behaved pets are welcome with their crates. Please tell us in advance if you're bringing a pet so we can plan your tent.

Is there Wi-Fi and mobile network?

Wi-Fi is available, but this is a remote hill village and speeds can vary. Mobile coverage is patchy on some networks, so download maps and tickets before you leave Munnar. Only Jio and BSNL have network coverage.

What can we do nearby?

Nearby waterfalls, tiger caves, trekking and hiking spots. Visit Pambadum Shola National Park, catch sunrise at Top Station, walk the strawberry and vegetable farms, and explore Kovilur village. Mattupetty Dam and Kundala Lake are on the way from Munnar.
