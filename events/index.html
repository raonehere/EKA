Slow Forest New Year

Welcome the New Year at Eka Forest, tucked away in the quiet mountains of Vattavada, near Munnar.

We're bringing together a small group of people for three days of music, nature, shared meals, conversations and unhurried moments.

Think slow mornings, beautiful mountain landscapes, evenings around the fire, music, and the simple joy of being together.

We've celebrated a forest New Year before, and we're coming back for another edition — with new experiences, familiar warmth and plenty of room to simply be.

Not another crowded New Year's party. Just a different way to begin the year.

Clay Sculpting Workshop

A small, hands-on gathering at Eka where we explored the simple art of working with clay and making a sculpture facilitated by Rajesh Rabberi.

Away from screens and everyday routines, we spent time shaping, creating and experimenting with our hands. No pressure to make something perfect, just the joy of learning, creating and spending time together.

A 3-days of earth, creativity and connection.

January 9–11, 2026 · Past

Flow state fitness retreat

Flow State was a three-day fitness retreat at Eka Forest, created around movement, strength, flexibility and reconnecting with the body conducted by Flow State Fitness team.

The experience included Kalari, yoga, mudgar, Animal flow, guided trekking and free-flow movement sessions.

Across three days, we explored movement in different forms from structured fitness practices to moving freely, breathing deeply and challenging ourselves outdoors.

It was an opportunity to step away from familiar routines, discover new ways to move and experience fitness in the middle of nature.
