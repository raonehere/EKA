Exclusive-use camp · Vattavada, Munnar

Bring your people to the woods

EKA Forest is a small tent camp in the eucalyptus and pine above Vattavada, the last Kerala village past Top Station. Take the whole camp — eleven tents, twenty-two people — for a company offsite, a facilitator-led retreat, a shoot, or a family gathering. We look after the tents, the food and the fire. You bring the people and the reason.

The camp

A whole camp, not a room block

Eleven canvas tents sit among the trees, each sleeping two. When you take all eleven, there are no other guests: the terrace, the garden, the fire pit and the views are yours.

Tents have warm bedding for cold nights. Washrooms are shared, clean and have hot water. Wi-Fi reaches the camp, though mobile signal is patchy — part of the point. Meals are cooked home-style in the farm kitchen, often with vegetables from Vattavada's terraces.

  • Eleven canvas tents, two guests each; single occupancy on request
  • Bedding, blankets and towels
  • Shared washrooms with hot water
  • Home-style breakfast, lunch and dinner — veg and non-veg, dietary needs on request
  • Campfire and stargazing, weather permitting
  • Farm, village and shola walks with a local guide
  • Parking, Wi-Fi, terrace and garden
  • Check-in from 1 PM; check-out by 11 AM
  • The whole camp, exclusively yours when you book all eleven tents

We keep the camp simple: this is a forest stay, not a resort. Paths are uneven, nights are cold, and the forest check-post closes at 6 PM. If your group needs something specific — a projector, a longer menu, a late check-out — ask early and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

Before you ask

Questions organisers ask

Can we book the whole camp?

Yes. Eleven tents, two guests each, up to twenty-two people. When you take all eleven, no other guests are booked in.

Is there a minimum group size?

Exclusive use is best value at 16–22 guests. Smaller groups can book the tents they need; tell us the headcount and we will quote honestly.

Can we bring our own facilitator?

Yes. Many groups do. We provide the venue, tents, meals and local logistics; your facilitator runs the programme.

Can you handle dietary needs?

Yes — tell us in advance. We cook simple home-style Kerala food, mostly vegetarian, with non-veg on request.

Is alcohol allowed?

Ask us before you plan. We will confirm what is allowed at the camp for your dates and group.

What about power, Wi-Fi and phone signal?

Wi-Fi is available; mobile coverage is patchy. If your offsite depends on reliable video calls, tell us and we will plan around it. Bring power banks.

How do we get there?

Vattavada is about 45 km and two hours from Munnar, past Top Station and through Pambadum Shola. The check-post is open 6 AM–6 PM. Share your arrival time and we will send the pin.

What is the advance and cancellation policy?

We set both out in your written quote before you pay anything. The standard direct booking uses a flat ₹2,000 advance; exclusive-use groups agree a percentage with us.

Do you host photo and film shoots?

Yes, by arrangement. Send the crew size, dates and a short brief; we will quote the camp and location use together.

Are pets and kids welcome?

Yes. Tell us in advance so we can plan tents and meals.

Is the camp accessible?

The camp is on uneven forest ground with shared washrooms. Tell us the access needs of your group and we will be honest about what works.

Can we visit before booking?

Yes, if dates allow. Message us to arrange a site visit or a video walkthrough.
