Before you book

How does the advance work?

The advance is 20% of your total, so it grows with your nights and your group. You reserve your nights, pay by UPI (scan the QR or use the ID), and upload the screenshot; we check it and confirm on WhatsApp. It comes off your final bill, which you settle at EKA Forest.

What if I need to change my dates?

Your confirmation includes a private link to move the booking yourself. Pick any free nights and the advance carries over, no charge, and you can do it more than once. Changes close 48 hours before check-in; after that, message us on WhatsApp.

How many people can come?

We have eleven A-frame tents and each sleeps two. A couple shares one, but a solo guest still gets a whole tent, so three people need two. Children under 5 are free and share your A-frame tent. Children aged 5 to 10 pay half, and everyone 11 or older pays the full ₹1,850 per night. Dinner, breakfast, trekking and campfire are included. Groups larger than 22 adults can still send a request. We check availability and send the price on WhatsApp before anything is confirmed.

What's included in the stay?

An A-frame tent with bedding, access to the shared washrooms with hot water, the garden and terrace, and the campfire in the evening. Meals are home-style and served on request, tell us in the notes if you'd like them.

When will my booking be confirmed?

Your dates are blocked the moment your payment screenshot reaches us, not when you fill the form. We check the credit by hand and confirm on WhatsApp, usually within a few hours, and always with your A-frame tent, directions and the map pin.

Can I pay the full amount now?

No need. The advance by UPI confirms the booking; the balance is settled when you're here.
