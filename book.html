Stay rules

  1. Forest and wildlife. EKA Forest is in a forest area next to Pambadum Shola National Park. Stay on the paths, don't go into the forest alone or after dark, and never feed or disturb animals.
  2. Drug-free zone. Narcotics and illegal substances are strictly banned. Anyone found with them will be asked to leave at once, without a refund, and may be reported to the police.
  3. Limited medical help. The nearest hospital is in Munnar, about 2 hours away by road. Bring any medicines you need and tell us about health conditions before you arrive. Walks and activities are at your own risk.
  4. Respect the forest. Fires only in the campfire pit set up by our staff. No loud music after 10 PM, no littering, and please carry plastic and other non-biodegradable waste back with you.
  5. Confirmation and ID. Your booking is confirmed only after we verify your payment. Every adult guest must show a valid government photo ID at check-in. The Pambadum Shola check-post closes at 6 PM, so plan to arrive before then.
