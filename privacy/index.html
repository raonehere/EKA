Privacy Policy

EKA Forest collects only the information we need to hold and manage your booking.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your stay dates, number of guests, and any preferences you tell us about.
  • ID proof and payment screenshots you send to confirm a booking.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram, email, or through this site.

How we use it

We use this information to confirm your stay, manage payments, prepare for your arrival, and meet legal requirements. We do not sell your information.

Who can see it

Your booking details are visible only to the EKA Forest team and the services we use to run bookings, such as our booking spreadsheet and Notion workspace. Payment screenshots are used only to verify your advance.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as needed for accounts and legal records. You can ask us to delete information we no longer need.

Your choices

To ask for a copy of your information, correct it, or ask us to delete it, message us on WhatsApp at +91 70122 93538.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs.
