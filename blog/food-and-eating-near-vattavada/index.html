Food at EKA Forest and eating near Vattavada

5 November 2026 · 3 min read

Food is simple, home-style cooking, served in the outdoor dining area. The menu changes with the season and with what is fresh, so we describe the way we cook rather than a fixed list.

Meals at EKA Forest

Breakfast and dinner are included in your stay. Lunch is optional and can be added for ₹250 per person for each lunch. We use vegetables from the farms around Vattavada whenever they are in season.

Tell us about any dietary needs or allergies when you book, and we will plan around them. Vegetarian food is available on request, and non-vegetarian food is served every day.

Eating in Vattavada

The village has only a few small shops, so choices outside the stay are limited. Most guests eat at the property and walk into the village for snacks or supplies. If you need a specific item, bring it with you.

Planning ahead

Supply from the farms and shops depends on weather and harvest, so we cannot promise a particular dish on a particular day. If there is something you need, tell us before you arrive, and carry snacks and water for the journey from Munnar.

Want to come and see it for yourself? Message us on WhatsApp with your dates and how many of you are coming. We'll reply with what's free.
