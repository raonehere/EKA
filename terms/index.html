Terms and Conditions

These terms apply to stays booked at EKA Forest, Vattavada, Kerala.

Bookings

A booking is confirmed only after we receive the ₹2,000 advance. The advance is non-refundable and comes off your final bill.

Rates

  • ₹1,850 per person, per night.
  • Includes dinner, breakfast, guided trekking, and the evening campfire.
  • Children aged 5 to 10 pay half (₹925). Children under 5 stay free.
  • Optional lunch is ₹250 per person.

The balance is paid at the camp. Rates may change for future dates.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 1 PM. Check-out is by 11 AM. The Pambadum Shola forest check-post closes in the evening, so please plan to cross it before then.

ID proof

We ask for a government-issued photo ID for each adult before confirming a booking. We may also ask for ID at check-in.

Large groups

Groups larger than 20 to 22 people are handled on WhatsApp. Availability and the final price are confirmed there.

During your stay

EKA Forest is an outdoor, nature-led stay. Weather, roads, and forest rules can change. Please follow our instructions and local rules, keep the property and surrounding farms clean, and respect other guests, staff, and neighbours.

Families, children, and well-behaved pets are welcome with prior notice. We may end a stay for unsafe or disruptive behaviour.

Liability

You are responsible for your personal belongings and for the behaviour of your group. Please tell us about any medical needs before you arrive.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kerala, India.
