Cancellation Policy

Please read this before paying the advance.

Advance

The ₹2,000 advance is non-refundable. It comes off your final bill when you stay.

If you cancel

If you cancel your booking, the advance is not returned.

Changing dates

Date changes are subject to availability. Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can. We will do our best to move your booking, but a change is not confirmed until we reply.

No-shows

If you do not arrive on your booked dates, we treat it as a cancellation and the advance is not returned.

If we cancel

If EKA Forest cannot host your stay, we will offer an alternative date or refund the advance.

Weather and access

Vattavada is a remote hill village. Heavy rain, fog, road closures, or forest check-post rules can affect travel. These are outside our control, so please plan extra time and travel in daylight.
